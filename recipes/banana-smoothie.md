# Banana Smoothie

**Category:** Drinks
**Difficulty:** Easy
**Serves:** 1
**Time:** 5 minutes

---

## Ingredients

- 1 ripe banana (frozen works great!)
- 1/2 cup milk (dairy or non-dairy)
- 1/2 cup plain yogurt
- 1 tablespoon honey or maple syrup
- 1/4 teaspoon vanilla extract
- Handful of ice (skip if using frozen banana)

---

## Instructions

1. Peel the banana and break it into chunks.
2. Add all ingredients to a blender.
3. Blend on high for 30–60 seconds until completely smooth.
4. Taste and adjust sweetness if needed.
5. Pour into a glass and serve immediately.

---

## Variations

- **Peanut Butter Banana:** Add 1 tablespoon of peanut butter.
- **Berry Banana:** Add a handful of frozen strawberries or blueberries.
- **Chocolate Banana:** Add 1 tablespoon of cocoa powder.
- **Green Banana:** Add a handful of baby spinach — you won't taste it!

---

## Tips

- Frozen bananas make the smoothie thicker and creamier — peel and freeze overripe bananas ahead of time.
- Use oat milk for a naturally sweeter flavour.
